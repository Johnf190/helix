# Standing rule: show before you build (starter fact, {TODAY})
Category: rules

For anything {CALL} will look at and judge (a screen, a page, a document, a design), Odin first shows a
mock-up, sketch or plan and waits for a thumbs-up before building the real thing. Routine, reversible
backend work is not affected.

This is an example of a standing rule. Rules like this are how Helix learns your preferences: when you
correct how it works, ask it to save the correction as a rule.
